# Cold Email Optimization - Tips from r/agency

**Source:** Reddit post by u/Zealousideal_Pop3072 — "4 months with Instantly for cold email: 0.8% to 5.1% reply rate"
**Saved:** July 1, 2026

## Core Insight: Infrastructure > Copy
The jump from 0.8% → 5.1% reply rate came primarily from fixing deliverability infrastructure, not rewriting email copy.

## Tooling & Infrastructure
- Consolidate inboxes (went from ~70 to fewer, manageable count)
- Tool stack: Lemlist ($700/mo) → Instantly (unlimited inboxes, better pricing)
- Use waterfall lead verification — Apollo alone had 30% bounce rate
- ESP matching, warmup, AI spintax for deliverability

## Messaging Strategy (80/20 Rule)
- 80% templated, 20% AI-generated for testing new offers/angles
- Use AI to make the offer relevant to individual prospect in the last 20%
- Avoid over-investing in Clay-style manual personalization — time spent didn't correlate with reply rate lift

## Scaling Bottlenecks (in order)
1. **Deliverability** — is the email even reaching inboxes?
2. **Lead Quality** — are they the right audience?
3. **Credibility & Copy** — do you understand what you're selling?
4. **Volume** — are you sending enough?
5. **Response Time** — after deliverability, response time becomes the next bottleneck

## Other Tips
- Use behavioral signals (LinkedIn job postings, funding announcements) vs just job titles
- LinkedIn profile visit 1 day before email warms up lead (~30% reply rate improvement)
- Cross-check leads with Bouncer/MillionVerifier to keep bounce rate low
- At scale, 5% reply rate is huge — don't chase 15%
- Test AI inbox manager for automated reply handling before scaling volume
